Before you film

Platform rules & word bans

Every clip site and cam site draws its lines in a different place. Something allowed on one store gets your clip pulled on another, and a single banned word in a title can bury it in search or get it rejected outright.

The best public reference I know is Sophie Ladder’s Site Restrictions sheet. It compares content rules across the major clip sites and live streaming platforms side by side, and keeps a running list of banned words per platform. Check it before you plan a shoot, and again before you write the title.

Rules change often and the sheet is a community effort. When something matters, confirm it in the platform’s own terms of service.

Template

The clip title formula

Search on clip stores is literal. The first words of your title carry the most weight, so lead with the keywords buyers type, then add the detail that makes your clip different.

Copy the template, swap in your own keywords, and check every word against the ban list first.

Title template

[Keyword 1] [Keyword 2] [Keyword 3]: [what happens, in buyer language] with [your name] ([format, e.g. POV])

Description template

[Your name] [does what] in this [length] [format] clip. [One sentence of setting and tension.] [One sentence naming the fetishes and acts, using your search keywords.] [One sentence on how it ends or what the viewer feels.] Download now and [call to action].
