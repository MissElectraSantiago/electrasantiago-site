I am Electra Santiago, a creator, a traveler, a performer, and a woman who has shaped her life around art, power, and the beauty of intentional desire.

I have spent the last year traveling between cities such as my native Seattle, as well as San Diego, Berlin, Melbourne, London, Tokyo, Paris, and more. I even stayed in a few places I discovered entirely by accident because they taught me something important. Traveling this much transforms you. It sharpens your edges, deepens your curiosity, and teaches you how to thrive anywhere. My work grew alongside every new country. My voice became stronger. My Femdom style evolved with every chapter of my life.

My journey has taken me to international events, gatherings, and communities across the world. My work has appeared in Darkside, Subspace, Demasque and Sinical, and on the Authentically Kinky podcast. See all my features.

My videos are visceral and raw. They live in the space where emotion sits close to the surface and power feels personal. I create in a way that feels immediate and alive, as if you are breathing inside the moment with me. Every frame carries tension, intention, and a kind of honesty that cannot be faked.

I am not interested in performance for its own sake. What I create, including my videos, my writing, my scripts, and my teaching, comes from experience, observation, and a form of control that is never loud or dramatic. I prefer quiet authority, deliberate pacing, psychological depth, and the sort of tension that grows inside the silence between words.

I have been creating content for more than five years while building a global audience of over 100k followers across platforms such as OnlyFans, Clips4Sale, Twitter, FetLife, and this site. People know me for my videos that feel intimate, immersive, and sharply personal, like you’re right there with me.

Outside of Femdom, I am deeply invested in art, especially sculpture, and I am developing a portfolio for a fine arts program here in Melbourne. I care about design, structure, and anything that requires intention, skill, and a touch of obsession. I am also developing my own strap on harness system; a long term project that blends engineering, kink, and aesthetic precision.

This site brings everything together, including my writing, my Femdom philosophy, my travel, my work, my ideas, my projects, and the evolution of everything I am creating. If you are here to explore, to learn, or to experience my world, welcome.

I am glad you found me.
