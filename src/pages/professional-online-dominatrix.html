Miss Electra Santiago

Online Dominatrix, femdom creator and artist, based in Melbourne.

I’m Electra Santiago, a professional online Dominatrix and femdom creator. I grew up in Seattle and now live in Melbourne, and I’ve spent the last year working from San Diego, Berlin, London, Tokyo, Paris and a few places I found entirely by accident.

I’ve been making content for more than five years, and over 100k people follow my work across OnlyFans, Clips4Sale, Twitter, FetLife and here. My videos are intimate and personal. I don’t do loud or theatrical. I prefer quiet authority, slow pacing, and the tension that builds while you wait for my next word.

You’ll find me at events like the German Fetish Ball and the International Femdom Summit in Romania. My work has appeared in Darkside, Subspace and Demasque, I’ve been interviewed by Sinical, and I joined the Authentically Kinky podcast to talk about power and the philosophy behind what I create. See all my features.

Outside of femdom, I’m an artist. I’m building a sculpture portfolio for a fine arts program in Melbourne, and I’m designing my own strap-on harness system, a long-term project where engineering, kink and design meet.

I also mentor other creators, helping them find where they’re stuck and build systems that fit how they actually work.

Whether you’re here to serve, to learn or just to look, welcome. I’m glad you found me.
